# 2026-11-26 — Layout and fonts

**In one line:** gave the site one reading rhythm, two fonts and an aligned layout on phone and web.

## What we set out to do
Better fonts, and well spaced, aligned text on both phone and web.

## What we built
Newsreader and Inter; a three-column web layout; phone tabs and a tidier header; job entries, lists, offer cards and
contact rows on a shared spacing scale; still tool logos; `ui-registry.md`.

## Dwayne's approach
- Named the real problem: text not structured, spaced or aligned.
- Asked for a recommendation, then picked option A from a clickable mockup.

## Claude's approach
- Showed three font pairs on the real content before building.
- Recommended a serif for headings (keeps the character) with a sans for body text (reads better on phones).
- Replaced the floating 3D logos: they overlapped once more tools were added. Still logos also load less code.

## What we learned
Alignment comes from deciding the columns once and making every block use them.
